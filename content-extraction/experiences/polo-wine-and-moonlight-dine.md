# Polo, Wine & Moonlight Dine

- **Location:** DELHI & RAJASTHAN, INDIA
- **Categories/Tags:** Unavailable

## Overview
Enjoy an afternoon playing "the game of kings" under the guidance of a high goal international polo player. Play a few chukkas or experience an introduction to this glamorous game at the hands of an expert. Then, end your evening with a delicious dinner under the stars.

## Inclusions
- Personal instruction from a world-class polo player at a private polo field.
- Feel the exhilaration of riding a highly trained polo pony.
- Begin on the back of a wooden horse and then play chukkas or stick and ball on the field.
- Finish with a short game for the new polo champion!
- Complete your evening with an exquisite dinner for two at the polo farm.
