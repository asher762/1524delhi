## Overview
Ahilya Fort in the town of Maheshwar is on the banks of the sacred River Narmada. Maharani Ahilya Bai Holkar ruled here from 1765 to 1796 and built Ahilya Wada. In 2000, Prince Richard Holkar, her descendant and son of the last Maharaja of Indore and his American wife, converted his home in Ahilya Wada into a guest residence, today known around the world as Ahilya Fort.

The hotel sits atop the massive sandstone walls of Maheshwar Fort. From the terrace, the garden and room balconies you can look upon the banks of the holy Narmada, where pilgrims come to immerse themselves and pray to Lord Shiva. Ahilya Fort has 17 unique rooms set in six 18th century buildings around a shady courtyard, a lovely large pool and a vegetable garden that is a work of art. This authentic 18th century fort provides a truly magical setting to experience Maheshwar's colourful festivals, the world famous Maheshwari textiles or, for the more adventurous, a day trip to the famous Omkareshwar temples and the deserted 15th century city of Mandu.

## Accommodation
Ahilya Fort has two Royal Suites, a Royal Tent and fourteen unique rooms set in six buildings of the 18th-century fort. All are distinctive. The suites feature private balconies overlooking the Narmada and the Royal Tent is set in a private garden. All suites and rooms are airconditioned.

- **Royal Suite** — The Royal Suites (02) are air conditioned and feature a king sized bed, a sitting room and a private balcony overlooking the river and temples.
- **The Arjun Royal Tent** — The Arjun Royal Tent is a luxurious tent that has a separate sleeping area, a sitting area and a large bathroom. It is enclosed in a private garden and has its own private plunge pool overlooking the river.
- **Superior Double Rooms and AC Tent** — The Superior Double Rooms are spread across different blocks. All rooms are airconditioned with distinct interiors. Some of these open into charming courtyards, have river-view alcoves, a charming turret, balcony or garden view. The hotel has one superior airconditioned tent.
- **Superior Twin Rooms** — The Superior Rooms are spread across different blocks. All rooms are airconditioned. Some of these open into charming courtyards or have a garden view.

### Festivals at Maheshwar
**The Sacred River**

Every year in February, Ahilya Fort presents The Sacred River, a celebration of the Narmada. Three days and nights of classical dance, vocal and instrumental concerts in stunning settings.

On Lord Shiva's birthday, the ghats are thronged with the faithful dressed in festival finery.

The observance of Muharram is the most colourful event in Maheshwar's crowded calendar of festivals.
