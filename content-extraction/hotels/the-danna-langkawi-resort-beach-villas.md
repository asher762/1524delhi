## Overview

Take your time to explore this luxury beachfront resort which features exclusive private pool villas, colonial oceanfront rooms and suites that boast views ranging from tropical mountains, ocean, marina and courtyard – featuring bespoke Jo Loves by Jo Malone CBE bath amenities. ## Dining
The Danna Langkawi houses a variety of restaurants and lounges offering local and international specialties. From casual to elegant dining, romantic dinner by the beach or an intimate family gathering, you will discover your perfect place to eat while you're in Langkawi.

- **Terrace Italian Restaurant** — Experience a touch of Italy at the Terrace as it showcases classic Italian dishes within a modern dining setting. Feast on favourites like fresh seafood and homemade pasta paired with an excellent selection of wines and cocktails. Open for lunch and dinner, you have a choice of enjoying your meal alfresco overlooking our infinity pool or within the more relaxed indoor seating.
- **Planter's — Breakfast Buffet, Asian & European Cuisine** — Discover the culinary delights at Planter's Restaurant, your all-day dining destination in Langkawi. Start your mornings with a vibrant bubbly buffet breakfast, followed by an enticing à la carte lunch menu featuring a global selection and locally inspired specialties like rendang, satay, and more. As night falls, immerse yourself in the rich flavors of Malaysia with our scrumptious Heritage Buffet dinner, offering a captivating array of traditional Malaysian dishes. Planter's is where exceptional dining meets unforgettable experiences.
- **Straits & Co — Authentic Peranakan Restaurant** — Savor the essence of Malaysian heritage at Straits & Co, The Danna Langkawi's authentic Peranakan restaurant. Enjoy a memorable dinner showcasing vibrant Peranakan flavors in a relaxed ambiance. Peranakan culture, rooted in the 14th-century migration of Chinese settlers to Southeast Asia, is known for its unique blend of Sino-Malay influences. This heritage shines through in colorful attire, intricate Nyonya dishes, and traditional music, offering a dining experience rich in history and flavor.
- **STC Café & Deli** — Escape to STC Café & Deli, where afternoons from 11am to 6pm are made for indulgence. Unwind with a perfectly brewed coffee or tea, and savor a selection of seasonal desserts and pastries crafted with the finest ingredients. Enjoy our Signature Afternoon Tea, complete with homemade scones in a cozy, inviting ambiance designed for leisurely moments.
- **Churchill Cigar Room** — With its elegant leather and wood decor furnished with bold tones, the Churchill Room offers cigar connoisseurs a refined retreat. Known as one of the best ventilated cigar lounges in the country, you can enjoy specially curated pairings of alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages with a selection of cigars that includes the room's namesake's favourite brand - Romeo y Julieta. Enjoy the finer things in life with the Curated Pairing Experience which pairs a chosen cigar with a high-end single malt whisky, aged rum, premium brandy or non-alcoholic beverage.
- **Verandah Classic Bar & Lounge** — Step back in time to the glamorous days of the 1920s at The Verandah Lounge. Known as one of Langkawi's classiest lounges, you'll be greeted with a visually stunning display of soaring high ceilings, magnificent chandeliers and towering pillars. This hidden gem is effortlessly stylish where you can unwind on plush sofas and indulge in nibbles and cocktails.
