## Overview
The Blackbuck Lodge sits in splendid seclusion along the Velavadar Blackbuck National Park in Gujarat's Bhal grassland region, near the west coast of the Gulf of Cambay. This wildlife resort offers unsurpassed luxury in the middle of an untamed landscape. In this majestic tapestry of the wild, The Blackbuck Lodge, lying in 70 acres of lush natural grasslands of the region, is an idyllic retreat for the discerning traveler.

Explore Velavadar National Park with its rich wildlife and birdlife, unwind in your luxury villa or visit places of historical interest e.g. Lothal (an important site of the Indus Valley Civilization). The Blackbuck Lodge is the perfect setting for family reunions, retreats and weekend getaways.

## Location
The Blackbuck Lodge is situated opposite Velavadar Village, near Velavadar National Park, Dist. Bhavnagar. It is a 60 minute drive from the Bhavnagar railway station or airport (65 km). The Ahmedabad airport is at a distance of 160 km (approximately 3 hrs driving time).

**How to get there**
- The nearest airport to Velavadar is Bhavnagar, which is 65 kms from Velavadar. Mumbai is connected to Bhavnagar through daily domestic flights. Ahmedabad airport is at a distance of 160 kms which is a 4-hour drive from Velavadar and it receives international and domestic flights.
- Nearest railway station is at the town of Dhala, which is about 50km from the park. Bhavnagar is the nearest railhead, connected by trains from Mumbai and Ahmedabad.
- The nearest town, Valabhaipur, is 32 km away, and Ahmedabad 145 km away. If you come by private vehicle, the Blackbuck Lodge is entered from Valabhaipur on the old highway or Adhelai on the Bhavnagar-Ahmedabad highway.

## Special Sections

### Game Drives
Spread over 34.52 sq km, Velavadar National Park has India's largest population of Blackbuck. Guests may also see the endangered Indian Grey Wolf, Nilgai or Blue Bull, Striped Hyena, Indian Fox, Golden Jackal and many other species of mammals.

Velavadar National Park and its nearby wetlands comprise an important Bird Area notified by Birdlife International. The park is a good place to watch grassland birds like Sandgrouse, Francolins, Quails, Saras Crane and Stolikza's Bushchat.

In winter Velavadar National Park hosts the world's largest harrier roost - Montagu's Harrier, Pallid Harrier and Marsh Harrier can be seen in large numbers.

The Blackbuck Lodge takes you on a game drive in its own fleet of customized 4X4 vehicles accompanied by professionally trained guides. The morning drive starts at dawn and usually lasts for about 2 hours and the evening drive starts at 4pm and also lasts about 2 hours. On prior intimation, full day drives can also be arranged.
