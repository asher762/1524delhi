## Overview
Antara River Cruises offers Indian and international clients a new way to experience travel on the rivers of South Asia, sailing along untouched destinations, visiting bustling cities, hidden villages, and serene waters, all the while unpacking only once.

Antara seeks travelers who are looking for untouched experiences on the region's life-giving river systems. We seek to offer rare vistas of contemporary South Asia, in all its antiquity and futurity, on ships that offer great creature comforts, warm service and graceful travel. Each handcrafted ship is uniquely designed to give passengers nested adventures. We achieve this by having a really high passenger to crew ratio of 36:40+ and guides and special lecturers who offer insight into the worlds that passengers encounter each day.

Beautiful textiles, art, cutlery, furniture, libraries, boutiques, wellness and spa and activities are themed to the cruises and make the stay onboard a sensorially rich experience. Each shore excursion is chosen to surprise, thrill and provoke the curious traveller.

## Location
Antara's Ganges ships dock in Kolkata and Varanasi, two great river cities on the Lower Gangetic plains. Our Kolkata jetties are located in the historic Botanical Garden in Howrah (founded in 1787) and Indenture Memorial Jetty in Kolkata. In Varanasi, guests embark at Ravidas Ghat, at the northern end of Varanasi's 84 ghats. In Orissa, guests embark at Gupti Jetty, Rajanagar, Kedrapara Dist.

**How to get there**
- All major international and domestic airlines connect to Kolkata, Varanasi, Bhubhaneswar
- Extensive rail network connecting major cities in India
- The embarkation points are 35-40 minutes from the nearest airport / railway station

## Accommodation
Antara Ganges Voyager I & II is a spacious, high-ceilinged 28 suites serene sanctuary, with beautiful hand-painted murals and all the comforts of a five-star hotel. All suites have a cosy sitting area and a French balcony with floor-to-ceiling glass doors. A bed dressed in soft linens, with a choice of pillows. The roomy bathroom has a rain shower, comfy robes and slippers, and spa-quality bath products. Other high-end amenities include a generous amount of closet space, under-bed storage, a flat-screen television with a wide selection of on-demand movies, individual climate control, bottled water replenished daily, fresh fruit and flowers upon arrival, an iPhone docking station with alarm clock, makeup mirrors and universal electrical plugs.

Antara Ganga Vilas is a celebration of India's post-independence design and a tribute to its innovations. Each room is inspired by Josef Albers' paintings which are experiments in the perception of color as well as tantra and its quest to perceive the many parts of the universe in its wholeness. Nauka Vilas has one bedroom while the Catamarans have 2 suites each with twin/queen beds.

- **Ganges Voyager** — Fleet of suite categories: Maharaja Suite (400 sq. ft., Persian rugs, Burma teak floors, rich tapestry, flat screen TV, on-demand movie service, writing desk); Viceroy Suites (colonial decor, Persian rugs, hand-stencilled walls, 360+ sq. ft., day bed, spacious bathroom); Heritage Suites (French balcony, four-poster bed, Persian rugs, floor-to-ceiling glass doors, hand painted walls); Colonial Suites (vibrant colours, picturesque prints, seating area, French balcony); Signature Suites (2 twin-size beds or 1 queen-size bed, seating area, floor-to-ceiling glass doors, hand-stencils, local murals, fine wood furniture).
- **Ganges Voyager II** — Same suite categories as Ganges Voyager: Maharaja Suite, Viceroy Suites, Heritage Suites, Colonial Suites, Signature Suites (see descriptions above).
- **Ganga Vilas** — 8 Suites on the Main Deck with French Balcony and 10 Suites on the Upper Deck with French Balcony. Every room comes with twin-size beds that can be converted to a queen-size bed, a seating area, writing desk, spacious built-in wardrobes, a flat screen LCD television and a non-alcoholic minibar. Bathrooms come with all-natural spa-quality bath products and modern bath fixtures including a rain shower. All suites are boutique with a unique colour and art adorning them.
- **Catamarans** — 2 Suites with Queen/Twin beds in one Catamaran. Antara Catamarans have suites designed with fresh contemporary furniture, original works of art on palm leaves, handloom textiles and luxe linen. Everything is designed for a beautiful night's rest in the heart of the forest.
- **MV Bengal Ganga** — The Bengal Ganga is an expedition river craft, made entirely out of Burma teak and iron. It sails from Kolkata to Munger and downstream to Kolkata between October and late March when the river's waters are plentiful yet calm.
