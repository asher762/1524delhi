## Overview
An award-winning luxury destination spa in the Himalayan foothills, Ananda is located on a 100-acre Maharaja's Palace Estate. Surrounded by graceful Sal forests, overlooking the spiritual town of Rishikesh and the Ganges river valley, Ananda integrates traditional Ayurveda, Yoga, and Vedanta with international wellness experiences, fitness, and healthy organic cuisine to restore balance and harmony.

Ananda was voted World's Best Destination Spa by Condé Nast Traveller, the UK in 2005, 2006, 2007 and 2012 and by Condé Nast Traveller, India in 2013, 2014, 2015 and 2017. Ananda was also voted the Best Spa for Mind, Body and Spirit, Yoga and Healthy Cuisine by Spa Finder Wellness Travel Awards 2015.

A blend of elegance with serenity, Ananda's guest rooms, suites, and villas are an abode of spirituality, solace, and opulence. The spa experience strives to achieve the ultimate harmony between the individual's physical and the mental realms. In order to preserve the tranquillity of the Destination Spa, children below the age of 14 years are not permitted at Ananda in the Himalayas.

## Location
Overlooking the Ganges, the Ananda is situated in the town of Narendranagar, Uttaranchal. It is a 40 minute drive from the Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun.

**How to get there**
- There are frequent flights from Delhi and Mumbai to the Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun followed by a 40 minute picturesque drive to Ananda in the Himalayas.
- It is a 4-hour journey on the Delhi-Haridwar route, followed by a one-hour drive to Ananda in the Himalayas.
- The drive to Ananda could take anywhere between 5-8 hours depending on traffic. Although many guests do choose to come by road, this is not recommended, as the traffic is difficult to estimate.

- **Ananda Suite** — Housed on the ground floor and overlooking the valley, the 3-bay Ananda Suite spells luxury and enchantment. The Ananda Suite also has an extra powder room with a shower in addition to the main bathroom.
- **Viceregal Lodge** — The Viceregal Suite with antique furniture and fittings includes a royal bedroom with a four-poster bed, large dining area and an expansive private terrace overlooking the landscaped estate gardens and the valley.
- **Ananda Villas** — Pantry service and a 40 sq.m private pool surrounded by a deck area and equipped with comfortable lounge beds.

## Wellness
**Ayurvedic Experiences**

An expansive spa spread across 25,000 sq. ft. with its 24 treatment rooms, state of the art gymnasium, outdoor temperature-controlled swimming pool and luxurious hydrotherapy facilities, is the centre of attention at Ananda. The spa featuring more than 80 body and beauty experiences integrates traditional Indian wellness practices of Ayurveda and Yoga with the best of international wellness experiences at the best destination spa in India.

The personalized therapy and activities program are designed to meet individual needs and health goals – de-stress, detoxification and cleansing, deep relaxation, anti-ageing, weight & inch loss, while also offering guidance in the areas of nutrition and active fitness. A team of qualified nutritionists, Western & Ayurvedic physicians, spa therapists, Yoga and Fitness experts all gently guide towards a better lifestyle at this Himalayan spa and wellness retreat.

- Ayurvedic Experiences: Ananda offers traditional ayurvedic dosha based consultation by Ayurvedic Doctors, full body therapeutic massages by expert therapists, and specialised Ayurvedic diet.
- Yoga, Meditation & Vedanta: Traditional Hatha Yoga is practiced at Ananda guided by expert Yoga teachers who will design a tailor made yoga and meditation program based on your goals.
- International Experiences: Revitalizing treatments including sensuous Aromatherapy, pressure points and tension release work through Reflexology, healing Tibetan Kuu Nye and many more.
- Healing Experiences: Himalayan Crystals cleansed at the source of the Ganges, bring their healing powers balancing the chakras and cleansing the being of negative thoughts and energy.
- Active Fitness: Detailed fitness consultation with body composition analysis and a personalised fitness training program using latest equipment and functional training.
- Physiotherapy: Comprehensive diagnostic assessment and treatment using advanced techniques & tools like Class IV LASERS, ZRT Matrix therapy, Joint alignment tools & Interferential therapy.

## Dining
Ananda's wellness cuisine incorporates key principles from Ayurveda, which takes into consideration not just taste, but suitability to body type. The cuisine comprises fresh & natural foods low in fat and calories with an emphasis on whole grains, fresh fruits and vegetables, lean proteins, low-fat dairy products, avoiding all artificial salts, colors, flavors, or preservatives. Ananda's wellness cuisine concept is reemphasized by the show kitchen at Ananda where the chefs instruct in how to make these dishes at home so that one may continue that 'way of life'.

- **The Restaurant** — The Restaurant offers a pleasant dining experience complete with a yantric decor and light strains of classical music. It specializes in the finest of Indian, Asian and European cuisines. The food is organic and fresh which helps in revitalizing your body. Our expert chefs also assist in helping each resident attain their specific dietary requirements which makes the Ananda dining experience truly special. The restaurant also includes a tree top deck and a pavilion for outdoor and intimate dining experiences.
- **The Tea Lounge** — The Tea Lounge in the Viceregal Palace is an ideal spot to spend a regal afternoon with tea and light snacks.
- **The Pool** — The Pool offers a cool, relaxed setting in which one can enjoy light healthy snacks that refresh and hydrate the body while offering a splendid view.
- **Private Dining** — Unique dining experiences are created in spectacular private venues to make your special occasion even more magical. The chefs at the Ananda will create a customized menu, perfect for the occasion.
