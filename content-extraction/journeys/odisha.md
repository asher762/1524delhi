# Cruising In Bhitarkanika with Antara River Cruises

- **Duration:** 4 days
- **Route/Stops:** Gupti → Dangamal → Habali Kathi → Gupti
- **Country:** India
- **Categories/Tags:** Unavailable

## Overview
The Antara cruise takes you to the heart of 'Bhitarkanika,' which means interior ('Bhitar') and exceptional or outstanding beauty ('Kanika'). With one of India's most significant wildlife populations, Bhitarkanika thrives as a vibrant ecosystem. Its rich history and natural wonders captivate nature enthusiasts, conservationists, and history lovers alike. Bhitarkanika's most distinguishing feature is its expansive mangrove ecosystem, one of the largest in India. Lying on the coast of the Bay of Bengal, Bhitarkanika, popularly referred to as the mini-Amazon, is the second-largest mangrove ecosystem in the country after the Sundarbans.

Antara's catamarans, MV Bhitarkanika and MV Mahanadi, are small boutique vessels with two plush, well-equipped cabins beautifully outfitted for comfort and efficiency. The catamarans are especially designed for river cruising in the Bhitarkanika National Park, Odisha, the world's second-largest mangrove system.

Traverse the Baitarani River systems and their tributaries from the comforts of your suite aboard the Antara catamaran. However, Bhitarkanika's allure doesn't stop at its mangroves. Its biodiversity, encompassing both terrestrial and aquatic realms, stands as a testament to its ecological significance.

Bhitarkanika is not just a sanctuary; it's a vibrant tapestry of life. Bhitarkanika Wildlife Sanctuary isn't just a realm of conservation; it's a living ecosystem where villages and communities have thrived for generations, intricately woven into the unique environment of this sanctuary.

## Daily Itinerary

### Day 1 — Gupti
Arrive at the village of Gupti, which is approx. 3½ hours drive from Bhubaneswar and a short distance from the nearby town of Rajnagar. You are welcomed on board with a refreshing beverage followed by a safety briefing. Enjoy sundowners as you gently traverse the Pathsala River enjoying a curated Hi-Tea. The sight of distant Bay of Bengal has breathtaking views. A multi course traditional meal awaits you in the evening giving you the flavours of the region. Overnight onboard Catamaran.

### Day 2 — Dangamal
Today sail into the rich and unique mangrove ecosystem with creeks, mudflats and estuaries to arrive in Dangamal. Disembark and visit the Orientation Centre. Then you also explore the park on foot to observe and learn about the mangrove forest on trails. Other residents of the sanctuary include water monitor lizards, king cobras, and migrating birds. About 62 different kinds of mangroves are found here. The virgin stretched beaches, meandering water courses, and tranquil surroundings intercepted by the chirping of birds, will surely enchant you. Enjoy a sundowner from the top deck of your cruise ship while cruising back to Gupti where the views of the forest and the setting sun are simply mesmerizing. Dinner at leisure followed by a relaxed evening. Overnight onboard Catamaran.

### Day 3 — Habali Kathi
After breakfast, set sail again to Dangamal. Ride the country boat in the narrow channel to Khola for birdwatching and viewing other animals along the river. Return to the Catamaran for lunch. Thereafter explore the park on foot on a different trail to observe and learn about the forest with birding watching opportunities. Cruise back to Gupti Jetty. Overnight onboard Catamaran.

### Day 4 — Gupti Disembark
Enjoy the sunrise and feel the fresh clear crisp air that is so invigorating. Relish the landscapes and beauty of the river and the life alongside. Disembark cruise at Gupti Jetty early morning post breakfast.
