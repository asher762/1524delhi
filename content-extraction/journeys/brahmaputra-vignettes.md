# Brahmaputra Vignettes

- **Duration:** 7 days
- **Route/Stops:** Guwahati → Tezpur → Silghat → Kaziranga → Guwahati
- **Country:** India
- **Categories/Tags:** Unavailable

## Overview
Assam, which literally means "a peerless land", is the gateway to Northeast India.

The journey features a river cruise aboard the luxury vessel ABN Charaidew II through the Brahmaputra river valley. The region offers blue rivers, green mountains with leopards and elephants, and aromatic tea gardens forming the world's largest tea-growing region.

The Brahmaputra is described as one of the greatest rivers in the Indian Subcontinent and is notably vast. A distinctive feature includes shifting sand islands that appear and disappear annually. A highlight involves a BBQ on these sandbanks under starlight.

The cruise combines temple visits, historic sites, and traditional Assamese village discovery. An optional extension adds a three-night stay at Diphlu River Lodge near Kaziranga National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its one-horned rhino population.

**About Diphlu River Lodge:** The lodge sits on Kaziranga's periphery, separated from the park by a narrow river where wild buffalos and elephants bathe. It features thatch-and-bamboo cottages on stilts inspired by Mishing tribal architecture, with handmade regional artisan weaves and artifacts throughout. The property prioritizes responsible tourism through greywater recycling, locally-sourced produce, and hiring naturalists from nearby villages.

## Daily Itinerary

### Day 1 — Guwahati
Meet at Guwahati airport for city sightseeing. Drive to Nilachal Hill to visit Kamakhya temple, one of Hinduism's most important sites, known for tantric rites. Board the cruise vessel and sail upstream for approximately one and a half hours to Kurua on the north bank.

### Day 2 — Cruising upstream
Spend the day cruising upstream with jungle-covered hills on either side, often battling strong currents. Pause to visit a riverside village with lush green vegetable and corn fields.

### Day 3 — Tezpur – Silghat
Sail to Tezpur. After lunch, visit the 6th century Da Parbatia temple ruins with beautifully carved portal. Explore town by rickshaw, visiting Cole Park featuring medieval stone carvings from throughout the region. Reboard ship at waterfront and continue upstream, passing below a new bridge spanning the vast riverbed, mooring at Silghat overnight.

### Day 4 — Kaziranga
Check out and drive 1 hour to Kaziranga National Park. Check into Diphlu River Lodge. After lunch, take afternoon jeep safari inside the National Park. Evening at leisure. Dinner and overnight at lodge.

### Day 5 — Kaziranga
Morning jeep safari inside Kaziranga. Return to lodge. After lunch, take afternoon jeep safari in another park range. Dinner and overnight at lodge.

### Day 6 — Kaziranga
Morning jeep safari inside the National Park. Return for lunch. Take afternoon jeep safari to another park range. Dinner and overnight at lodge.

### Day 7 — Departure
After breakfast, take jeep safari inside the National Park. Return to lodge and check out.
