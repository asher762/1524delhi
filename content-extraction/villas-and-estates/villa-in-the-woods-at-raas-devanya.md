## Overview
Welcome to The Villa in the Woods, a hidden gem nestled amidst the serene beauty of the hill slopes between Bhimtal and Mukteshwar. This enchanting three-bedroom homestay is part of the larger Devānya project, which embraces sustainable engineering and an eco-conscious lifestyle.

The property offers an escape from city life with luxuries guests are accustomed to, and features attentive staff.

## Highlights
- Set amongst ancient Deodar, Chide and Oak trees, with rooms peeking out of the treetops
- Sustainable Luxury: This homestay is part of the larger sustainably engineered, eco-conscious Devānya project
- The villa has three cosy and well-appointed bedrooms with warm, attentive service

## Location
Located between Bhimtal and Mukteshwar (via Padampuri) in the Himalayan forests, at an altitude of about 6700 feet, with easy access from Delhi.

**How to get there**
- 2.5 hours from the Pantnagar airport, which is serviced daily by Indigo from New Delhi
- Train journey of 5 hours 50 minutes from Delhi to Kathgodam, with a 75 minute drive from Kathgodam
- 6.5 hours to reach Devānya by car from Delhi
- Devānya's helipad is available to guests for use

## Accommodation
Three bedrooms across three levels.
- **Master Bedroom** — Located on the upper level, one of two bedrooms on this level.
- **Second Bedroom (upper level)** — Located on the upper level alongside the master bedroom.
- **Third Bedroom** — On the lower level, ideal for children.

Living and dining are on the middle level, with decks offering panoramic views of the surrounding hillside.
