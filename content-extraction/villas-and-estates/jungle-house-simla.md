## Overview
Perched on a sunlit spur at 7200 ft on Jakhu Hill, Jungle House commands sweeping views of the middle Himalayas. The property is the Simla home of HH Princess Kusum of Bharatpur, with an illustrious history that dates back to the late 19th century. It features two exquisite suites in a stone cottage and offers unsurpassed levels of privacy, luxury and service.

## Location
Jungle House is located on Jakhu Hill in Shimla, a mere 500 meters from Jakhu Temple and an easy 30 minute walk from The Mall (3km).

**How to get there**
- Shimla Airport (Jubbarhatti) is approximately 25 km from Jungle House (a 60 minute drive).
- Shimla Railway Station is approximately 6 km away (a 20 minute drive).
- Shimla is within easy driving distance from Chandigarh and Delhi.
